# Learning Goals

**Status:** BASELINE

## Primary goal

Build the English competence and adaptive learning habits needed for IELTS Academic and real study-abroad participation.

## Goal dimensions

- IELTS overall and per-skill targets, never only an overall band.
- Intended study level and academic context.
- Deadline and its uncertainty.
- Available minutes per day and days per week.
- Current competency profile by modality.
- Language, academic, and study-abroad readiness gaps.

Goals are versioned. Changing a target or deadline changes planning, gaps, forecasts, and the roadmap; it does not rewrite historical evidence.

## Learning outcomes

The system develops:

- language knowledge: grammar, vocabulary senses, chunks, functions, discourse, and pronunciation knowledge;
- receptive skills: listening and reading under varying speed, accent, genre, and processing load;
- productive skills: writing and speaking with accuracy, complexity, fluency, independence, and interaction;
- academic skills: note-taking, summarizing, synthesizing, seminar participation, and academic task handling;
- exam performance: format knowledge, timing, strategy, and calibrated benchmark performance;
- metacognition: planning, monitoring, self-correction, reflection, and responsible support use.

## Evidence standard

Strong claims require sufficient, trustworthy evidence across independent performance, appropriate difficulty, novelty, transfer, and delayed retention. Promotion thresholds vary by competency type; a recognition item cannot prove productive mastery.
