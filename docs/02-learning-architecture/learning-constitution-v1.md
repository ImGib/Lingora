# Learning Constitution v1

**Status:** FROZEN

These articles constrain every implementation and policy.

## Article 1 — Preserve semantic layers

Content -> Performance -> Evaluation -> Observation -> Evidence -> Learner State -> Decision is traceable lineage, not one score. LearningClaim != Competency; exposure != practice opportunity != evidence opportunity. Assessment produces observations; it does not own mastery or the next action.

## Article 2 — Respect uncertainty

Unknown is not weak. Evidence existence is not sufficiency; historical evidence is not current confidence. Conflicting, insufficient, stale, low-trust, or contaminated evidence must remain identifiable. Do not manufacture certainty with a single percentage.

## Article 3 — Separate knowledge and performance

Recognition, controlled production, free production, automaticity, and real-time performance are different. State is tracked by competency and modality. A transfer failure does not automatically erase knowledge.

## Article 4 — Interpret support

Hints, replay, transcript, dictionary, translation, models, sentence starters, and AI suggestions alter evidence independence. Correctness under support may guide teaching but cannot be treated as independent mastery.

## Article 5 — Require durability and transfer

Immediate post-instruction success is not mastery. Strong promotion requires delayed retention and success at sufficient transfer distance across ItemFamilies/contexts appropriate to the competency. Forgetting is not the same as never learned.

## Article 6 — Diagnose before labeling

An error is an event, not a weakness. A weakness needs repeated, attributable evidence. A bottleneck additionally requires goal relevance and critical-path impact.

## Article 7 — Feedback must close a loop

Feedback is not learning; correction is not mastery. Feedback must support attention, self-correction, targeted recovery, retry/rewrite, transfer, and later verification. Limit feedback to a cognitively manageable priority set.

## Article 8 — Protect learner authorship

AI output, copied models, and inserted suggestions are not learner production. Contribution provenance must prevent assisted text from becoming strong independent evidence.

## Article 9 — Protect the learner from system failure

Technical, content, audio, ASR, or evaluator failure is not learner failure. Invalid sources can quarantine/revoke derived evidence and trigger recomputation without deleting history.

## Article 10 — Sustain learning

Breaks, workload limits, novelty budgets, consolidation, light days, and safe return after absence are architectural constraints. Deadline pressure cannot justify unlimited workload or abandoning protected foundations.

## Article 11 — Keep the learner moving

Every valid state must have at least one valid next action. Skipped is not failed; paused is not abandoned; a blocked path must offer recovery, alternative modality, prerequisite work, resume, or safe completion.

## Article 12 — Keep authority explainable

Hard constraints, vetoes, soft objectives, selected action, and reason trace must be distinguishable. The system may forecast a range but must not promise a band or silently change the learner's target.

## Article 13 — Govern change

Curriculum, competency graphs/claims, content/item families, evaluators, evidence/decision policies, goals/targets, and feature releases are versioned. Historical attempts and consequential decisions keep the versions used; explicit reprojection never rewrites their facts.

## Article 14 — Preserve privacy and access boundaries

Learners own access to their data but do not author derived intelligence. Speaking recordings and writing artifacts have explicit retention, access, export, and deletion behavior.

## Article 15 — Separate learner and system learning

The Learner Learning Loop changes capability through instruction, practice, feedback, retry, transfer, and retention. The System Learning Loop improves content, evaluators, policies, and interventions through governed evidence. System learning cannot silently relabel learners or publish unvalidated changes.

## Article 16 — Preserve agency without falsifying state

Skip, defer, replace, and explore are legitimate learner choices. DailyPlan owns sequence and NextAction is its preferred executable projection; overrides change orchestration, not historical performance or capability.
