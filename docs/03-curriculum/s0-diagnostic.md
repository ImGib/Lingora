# S0 — Diagnostic Curriculum

**Status:** VALIDATED

## Purpose

S0 builds a usable learner profile and chooses the safest next action. It does not assign one global level, exhaust the learner, or require a two-hour placement test.

## Inputs

- Goal, target profile, deadline, intended study context.
- Native language, prior learning, confidence, accessibility needs.
- Time availability, device/audio conditions, and learner consent.

## Adaptive probes

1. Learning profile and orientation.
2. Grammar: recognition, recall, controlled use, and brief production where possible.
3. Vocabulary: common senses/chunks; avoid obscure-word trivia.
4. Reading: decoding, explicit meaning, inference, vocabulary in context, and processing load.
5. Listening: first-pass comprehension, segmentation, explicit detail, replay dependency, and audio quality.
6. Writing: task understanding, sentence control, idea development, cohesion, planning, and independent production.
7. Speaking: comprehension, production, fluency, interaction, pronunciation opportunity, and audio/ASR confidence.
8. Pronunciation: perception and production are separate.

## Operating rules

- Use branching and stopping rules when enough evidence exists or fatigue rises.
- Start with low-risk probes, then seek the most discriminating next observation.
- Preserve first-pass performance before offering support.
- Do not treat skipped, unavailable modality, microphone failure, low audio quality, or insufficient production as a learner failure.
- Topic familiarity and world knowledge are potential confounds.
- Assessment items remain separated from instruction and protected from leakage.

## Output

S0 produces:

- competency/modality estimates with confidence and sufficiency;
- known unknowns and untested areas;
- suspected issues, never premature weaknesses;
- support/dependency signals;
- prerequisite gaps and safe bridge recommendations;
- an initial workload profile;
- the first roadmap version and an immediately valid next action.

## S0 -> S1

Learners do not “fail placement.” A beginner starts with an accessible S1 entry or pre-foundation bridge; a stronger learner may test out of specific units while retaining verification/review opportunities. The transition must never dead-end.
