# S1 — English Foundation

**Status:** VALIDATED

## Purpose

S1 establishes usable foundational English across connected skills. It is not a grammar course with listening and speaking added later.

## Integrated foundation sequence

| Unit | Orientation | Representative language and performance |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Survival English | classroom/help language, basic identity, instructions, core sounds and decoding |
| F2 | Self & Identity | be/have, personal information, spelling/names, short interaction |
| F3 | Daily Life | Present Simple, routines/time, third-person forms, first-pass daily-life listening |
| F4 | People & Places | description, there is/are, location, conjunctions, short connected writing |
| F5 | Past & Time | Past Simple, sequence, recounting, early discourse organization |
| F6 | Plans & Possibility | future intentions, can/could, requests, planning and interaction |
| F7 | Experience & Connection | experience language, comparisons, reasons, connected paragraph/speaking |
| F8 | Foundation Integration | limited new grammar; integrated retrieval, transfer, and verification |

The exact grammar inventory remains a map across units, not “one tense = one lesson.” Each concept progresses from meaning and form to independent use and later transfer.

## Continuous tracks

- Grammar: subject/verb system, tense/aspect foundations, negation/questions, noun phrase basics, modality, connection.
- Vocabulary: high-frequency senses, useful chunks, word families, collocations, and topic contexts under admission control.
- Pronunciation: perception -> controlled production -> intelligibility in connected speech; prioritize likely Vietnamese-L1 interference without assuming it for every learner.
- Listening: careful input -> natural variation; explicit details -> segmentation/inference; replay-supported -> first-pass.
- Reading: decoding and explicit meaning -> connected short texts, reference, inference, and extensive-input habits.
- Writing: word/sentence control -> connected sentences -> paragraph planning, drafting, self-review, and rewrite.
- Speaking: formulaic survival language -> short turns -> connected descriptions/narratives -> interaction, clarification, and spontaneous retrieval.

## Instruction and load

- English is primary; Vietnamese is optional scaffolding and gradually faded.
- Limit simultaneous novelty in form, vocabulary, task, topic, modality, and interface.
- Input difficulty and task difficulty are controlled separately.
- Worked examples fade; recognition does not substitute for recall/production.
- Interleaving begins only after initial discrimination is stable.
- Productive sessions balance accuracy, complexity, fluency, and fatigue.

## Completion profile

S1 completion is a readiness profile: protected foundations sufficiently established, priority competencies independently usable at required depth, review debt manageable, transfer sampled, and an accessible S2 path available. It is not 100% lesson completion.
