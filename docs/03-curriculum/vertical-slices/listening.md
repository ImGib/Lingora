# Validated Vertical Slice — Listening

**Status:** VALIDATED  
**Reference competency:** `LISTEN.EXPLICIT_TIME`

## Learner outcome

The learner identifies explicit time information in short spoken English under appropriate speed/accent/load and links it to the correct event, first with structured support and later on first listen in a novel audio.

## Audio contract

Audio, transcript, and questions are separate versioned objects. Audio has speaker/accent, speed, duration, signal quality, lexical/syntactic load, connected-speech features, and timestamp metadata. Transcript reveal is a recorded boundary event.

## Slice

1. Prepare activates context without revealing the answer.
2. First listen is preserved before replay/support.
3. Learner answers a low-reading-load question.
4. Replay state records count, speed changes, pauses, and segments.
5. Targeted listening and hints progress from attention direction to segment replay, phonetic/semantic cue, transcript excerpt, then full transcript.
6. Transcript analysis connects heard form, written form, segmentation, and meaning.
7. Micro-listening addresses connected speech, number/time confusions, or word boundaries.
8. Reconstruction/shadowing may train perception/processing but do not prove spontaneous speaking.
9. Transfer uses a different voice, wording, time expression, and context.
10. Delayed verification tests first-pass performance.

## Evidence

Evidence records first-listen versus replay-assisted success, support, accent/speed, novelty, memory load, opportunity salience, and confidence. Correct after transcript reveal is not independent listening evidence. Unlimited replay is a learning option with dependency signals, not invisible success.

## Diagnosis and recovery

Potential causes include unknown language, failure to recognize known language in speech, segmentation, connected speech, number discrimination, competing details, attention, or working-memory load. The next action targets the cause and can switch to text comparison or a lower-load audio. Bad audio, playback failure, or ambiguous items create technical/content events, not negative learner evidence.
