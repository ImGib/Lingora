# Validated Vertical Slice — Writing

**Status:** VALIDATED  
**Reference outcome:** produce and revise a coherent foundation paragraph

## Model

Writing is composite: task understanding, idea generation, planning, sentence formation, grammar, vocabulary, cohesion, organization, revision, and production independence. “Write 100 words” is a task condition, not a competency.

## Slice

1. Entry gate checks prerequisite sentence and language capabilities.
2. Learner interprets the prompt and plans ideas/organization.
3. Annotated models reveal decisions; they are not templates to copy.
4. Notice and worked examples focus on a narrow target.
5. Controlled activities practice idea development, sentence connection, ordering, and topic sentences.
6. Learner produces Draft 1 independently with support/contribution provenance.
7. Evaluation pipeline separately analyzes task fulfilment, organization/cohesion, vocabulary, grammar, mechanics, and evidence opportunities.
8. Learner self-reviews before AI correction.
9. Feedback selects a manageable priority set and explains what, why, and next action.
10. Targeted recovery precedes Draft 2.
11. Version comparison records uptake without treating a copied correction as mastery.
12. A new prompt verifies transfer; a delayed task verifies retention.

## Artifacts and authorship

Each draft is an artifact version. Learner, AI-suggested, AI-inserted, copied-model, and unknown contribution spans remain distinguishable. AI-generated text cannot become independent learner-production evidence.

## Evaluation integrity

Foundation feedback uses qualitative dimensions rather than false IELTS-band precision. AI evaluator/provider/model/prompt versions, confidence, disagreement, and errors are recorded. Insufficient production creates a supported next action, not an unjustified score.

## Recovery

Feedback is target-first, not an exhaustive redline. A grammar problem, weak idea development, planning gap, or template dependency receives a different recovery path. Success after feedback is `RECOVERING`; resolution needs novel independent production and later verification.
