# Content Coverage Roadmap

Coverage is tracked as a matrix, not a package count:

`communicative goal × claim × modality × opportunity type × independence × context × item family × retention/transfer condition`.

For each claim, report `missing / exposure-only / practiced / independently observed / retained / transferred`. Exposure volume cannot fill an evidence gap. Content inventory must also include provenance, review state, accessibility, asset health and quarantine status.

M1's concrete minimum is defined in [M1 content coverage](../m1-four-week-learning/content-coverage.md). Later coverage is opened only after M1 gaps and learner outcomes are measured.
