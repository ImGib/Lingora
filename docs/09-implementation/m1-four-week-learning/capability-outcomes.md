# M1 Capability Outcomes

- **C1 Personal information:** understand and communicate name, location, work/study and basic personal facts.
- **C2 Daily routine:** understand and describe common actions and basic times.
- **C3 People:** describe familiar people, jobs, characteristics, preferences and routines.
- **C4 Needs/preferences:** use simple forms such as “I want/need/like…”, “Can I…?” and “I'd like…”.
- **C5 Simple input:** identify main meaning and selected explicit details in short beginner written/spoken input.
- **C6 Independent production:** produce short original sentences, messages and spoken responses with decreasing support.

Claims beneath these outcomes specify meaning/form, modality, context, support and independence. Outcomes are earned from sufficient claim coverage; they are not inferred from course completion.
