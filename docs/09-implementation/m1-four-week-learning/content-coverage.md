# M1 Content Coverage

Minimum coverage for each weekly communicative goal:

- reviewed meaning/form examples and core vocabulary;
- sound/pronunciation awareness tied to real words;
- short reading and listening input with first-pass/support tracking;
- guided practice plus at least two independent ItemFamilies where the claim needs breadth;
- small original writing and speaking artifacts with contribution provenance;
- one near-transfer opportunity and one delayed verification where retention is claimed;
- feedback, a verification probe, targeted recovery and a new independent opportunity;
- accessible alternatives and declared degraded behavior.

Content moves `DRAFT -> REVIEW -> READY -> PUBLISHED`; health may become `SUSPECT -> QUARANTINED -> FIXED/RETIRED`. Quarantine removes future selection, identifies affected attempts/evidence and triggers review/reprojection without deleting history.

Do not fill 28 days with 28 days of new material. Day 6 is consolidation and Day 7 is light/rest/catch-up by default.

## Coverage matrix required before release

| Outcome | Exposure | Guided practice | Independent | New family/context | Delayed check | Minimum modalities |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 Personal information | required | required | required | required | required | read, listen, write, speak |
| C2 Daily routine | required | required | required | required | required | read, listen, write, speak |
| C3 People | required | required | required | required | initial | read, listen, write, speak |
| C4 Needs/preferences | required | required | required | required | initial | listen, read, speak; short write |
| C5 Simple input | required | supported response | first-pass tasks | new speaker/text | required | reading and listening separate |
| C6 Independent production | examples only | required | required | new prompt | required for selected claims | writing and speaking separate |

`Required` means at least one reviewed opportunity exists; it does not mean evidence is automatically sufficient. Claim-level authoring must specify how many distinct families/contexts and what delay are needed by the current evidence policy.

## Content inventory fields

Every M1 item/package must expose enough metadata to audit: communicative goal, claim, opportunity type, modality, context, authored difficulty, ItemFamily, expected independence, allowed support, transfer distance, content version, provenance/trust tier, accessibility assets, QA status and health status. Empirical difficulty remains separate from the author's estimate.
