# M1 Curriculum Entry and Rhythm

## Starting Point Check

Before Day 1, offer a short, optional check limited to M1-supported claims. It samples simple personal/routine vocabulary, `be`, basic Present Simple meaning/form, short reading/listening and one tiny production prompt. It is not adaptive placement and does not emit a level or IELTS prediction.

Output:

- `Already demonstrated`: valid evidence exists for the sampled condition;
- `Need more evidence`: unknown or insufficient coverage;
- `Recommended starting point`: a planning suggestion the learner can override.

Skipping starts at Day 1 with `UNKNOWN`, never `WEAK`.

## Weekly rhythm

```text
Acquire -> Practice -> Expand -> Apply -> Transfer
-> Consolidate -> Light / Rest / Catch-up
```

The planner may resume, shorten, defer or replace work without marking the learner failed. Do not fill 28 days with 28 days of new material.

## Weekly map

| Week | Communicative goal | Weekly proof |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Me & My English | introduce yourself; understand an introduction | unseen profile comprehension + independent self-introduction |
| 2 — My Daily Life | describe and understand routines | self-routine plus another person's routine in a new family/context |
| 3 — People Around Me | describe familiar people | transfer Present Simple into a person/family context |
| 4 — Needs, Preferences & Integration | handle a simple everyday exchange | integrated “Meet a new classmate” challenge |
