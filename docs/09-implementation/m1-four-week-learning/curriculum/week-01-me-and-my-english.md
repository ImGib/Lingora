# Week 1 — Me & My English

**Communicative goal:** introduce yourself and understand a simple introduction.

Language and skills: `be`, subject pronouns and basic questions; personal vocabulary; alphabet/name spelling and basic word stress; short profile reading/listening; 3–5 original written sentences and a 20–30 second spoken introduction.

| Day | Episode intent | Core experience | Evidence posture |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the language | meaning of simple introductions; personal words; hear/read examples | exposure only |
| 2 | Build supported forms | `I am / You are / He-She is`; guided profile completion | practice |
| 3 | Names and key details | alphabet/name spelling; first-pass short introduction | listening/reading observation |
| 4 | Apply | ask/answer basic personal questions | controlled independent opportunity |
| 5 | Produce | write 3–5 original sentences; record 20–30 seconds | independent artifacts |
| 6 | Transfer and consolidate | unseen person/profile and a new prompt | near transfer |
| 7 | Light/recover | due review, catch-up or rest; optional learner choice | no penalty for rest |

Week 1 implementation is specified by [M1 Slice 01](../slices/01-entry-and-week-1.md).
