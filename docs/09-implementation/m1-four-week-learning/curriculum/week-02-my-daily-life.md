# Week 2 — My Daily Life

**Communicative goal:** describe and understand a basic daily routine.

Language and skills: Present Simple meaning/forms, third-person singular and basic negatives; routine/time/day vocabulary; third-person `-s` pronunciation; short schedule/audio; routine writing/speaking.

| Day | Episode intent | Core experience | Evidence posture |
|---:|---|---|---|

| 11 | Read a schedule | explicit details and simple time reference | reading opportunity |
| 12 | My routine | short writing and spoken account | independent production |
| 13 | Another person's routine | third-person singular in a novel ItemFamily | transfer opportunity |
| 14 | Light/recover | delayed Week 1 verification plus Week 2 repair if needed | retention probe |
