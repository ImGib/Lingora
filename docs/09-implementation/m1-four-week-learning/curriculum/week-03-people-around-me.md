# Week 3 — People Around Me

**Communicative goal:** describe familiar people and understand basic descriptions.

Language and skills: `be`, `have`, Present Simple, possessives and basic adjectives; family/jobs/likes vocabulary; profile reading/listening and short description writing/speaking.

| Day | Episode intent | Core experience | Evidence posture |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 15 | People and relationships | family, jobs and familiar-person input | exposure + review |
| 16 | Describe people | `be`, `have`, possessives and adjectives | guided practice |
| 17 | Understand a person | short profile/audio; identify explicit information | reading/listening opportunity |
| 18 | Compare contexts | reuse Present Simple for a person, not own routine | contextual transfer |
| 19 | Produce description | original short writing and recording | independent artifacts |
| 20 | Verify and recover | novel probe, then targeted micro-lesson only if supported | verification/recovery |
| 21 | Light/recover | delayed Week 2 review, catch-up or rest | retention probe |
