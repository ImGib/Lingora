# M1 Evidence Strategy

Progression: exposure → guided practice → controlled independent use → independent use → transfer → delayed verification. Only the last four can become evidence when conditions are interpretable.

Each evidence opportunity records claim, modality, context, item family/exposure, difficulty, allowed/used support, response revision, expected independence, transfer distance, delay, technical validity, content/evaluator/policy versions and lineage to performance/observation.

Sufficiency remains multidimensional: `quantity, independence, breadth, recency, retention, transfer, trust, validity`. It is never compressed into a mastery percentage. Learner-facing labels may be `Unknown`, `Getting started`, `Developing`, `Getting consistent`, `Needs a refresh`, with plain-language uncertainty.

Immediate feedback may use one observation; recovery should first use a short verification probe; weekly progression requires varied opportunities; any high-impact placement/readiness decision requires broader independent and delayed evidence. State hypotheses must name possible falsifiers—for example, failure on a novel independent probe can weaken “consistent in familiar contexts,” while a single failure cannot prove “weak.”

Retention distinctions: historical success, current confidence, due verification, stale evidence, failed retrieval and successful relearning remain separate. Feedback uptake or corrected work shows response to support, not independent learning; a new prompt is required.

## Measurement validity and reliability

Before an opportunity can support a claim, check:

- **construct alignment:** the response actually requires the targeted capability;
- **condition interpretability:** support, exposure, timing, modality and technical conditions are known;
- **scoring validity:** evaluator output has an appropriate version, confidence and scope;
- **reliability for the decision:** breadth/repetition is adequate for the decision's impact;
- **confounds:** literacy, device/audio quality, copied/model text or unrelated vocabulary load do not dominate the result.

A valid narrow observation can still be insufficient for a broad decision. An apparently reliable repeated-item score can still be invalid evidence of transfer.

## Decision burden by horizon

| Horizon | Permitted use | Minimum burden |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate | feedback or retry suggestion | one technically valid observation; no trait claim |
| Session/day | choose review/practice/continue | multiple signals or explicit uncertainty; avoid one-item overreaction |
| Episode/week | progress emphasis or recovery | coverage across relevant claims and more than one family/context |
| Milestone/high impact | acceleration, placement/readiness | independent, varied, recent evidence with retention/transfer where claimed; return insufficient evidence otherwise |

## Writing and speaking v0

Writing captures an immutable learner artifact/version and contribution provenance. Deterministic checks, structured self-comparison or later human review may generate limited observations; no AI score is required. Speaking captures the original audio as the primary artifact, playback and guided self-check. Any transcript is derived support, not pronunciation evidence. Microphone, upload or ASR failure invalidates the opportunity rather than the learner.
