## Curriculum-wide rules

- Communicative goals organize learning; grammar and vocabulary serve those goals.
- Every week includes acquisition, guided practice, independent use, transfer, consolidation and a light/rest/catch-up path.
- Day numbers express pedagogical sequence, not punitive calendar locks.
- `Unknown != Weak`, `Skipped != Failed`, and technical failure creates no negative learner evidence.
- M1 does not claim CEFR A1, fluency or an IELTS band.

The final integrated challenge is described in Week 4. Evidence requirements and coverage live separately in [evidence strategy](evidence-strategy.md) and [content coverage](content-coverage.md).
