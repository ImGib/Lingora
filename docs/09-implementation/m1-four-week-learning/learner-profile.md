# M1 Learner Profile

Target learner: beginner, “mất gốc” or uncertain foundation; may recognize common words or fragments of grammar. Do not assume IPA knowledge, a stable grammar base, natural-speech comprehension, paragraph writing, sustained speaking or IELTS familiarity.

Availability ranges from 15–180 minutes/day. A minimum viable learning day may be about 10–15 minutes of priority review plus one core continuation. Longer availability becomes multiple sessions with breaks, not one 180-minute block.

Day 0 begins with `UNKNOWN`, never an all-weak profile. The optional Starting Point Check reports `already demonstrated`, `need more evidence`, and a recommended start. Skipping it is not failure.
