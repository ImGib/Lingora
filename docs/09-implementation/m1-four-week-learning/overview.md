# M1 — 4-Week Learning MVP

## Outcome

After 28 continuous days, a beginner can understand and independently communicate basic personal information and everyday routines across short reading, listening, writing and speaking contexts, with initial retention and transfer evidence.

M1 proves meaningful learning continuity, not lesson volume, CEFR level, fluency or IELTS band.

## Four learning episodes

1. **Week 1 — Me & My English:** introduce yourself and understand a simple introduction.
2. **Week 2 — My Daily Life:** describe and understand a basic routine.
3. **Week 3 — People Around Me:** describe familiar people and understand basic descriptions.
4. **Week 4 — Needs/Preferences & Integration:** express needs/preferences and complete the integrated “Meet a new classmate” challenge.

Each week follows Acquire → Practice → Expand → Apply → Transfer → Consolidate → Light/rest/catch-up. The planner adapts this rhythm; it does not hard-code calendar punishment.

## Scope

**NOW:** reviewed 28-day curriculum; Starting Point Check; basic vocabulary/grammar/sounds, listening/reading, small writing/speaking; review, recovery, planner and integrated challenge; valid opportunity/evidence capture.  
**PROTECT:** versioned policies, stronger calibration, full artifact provenance, content quarantine, privacy/security/accessibility and degraded modes.  
**LATER:** universal SRS, full IPA course, generative tutoring, automated writing/speaking scores, IELTS prediction.

Implementation begins with the concrete [Entry and Week 1 vertical slice](slices/01-entry-and-week-1.md) only after M0/01F closure. Later M1 slices are not pre-generated; their boundaries respond to measured coverage and learner-flow results.
